Current Gaps in AHCA Medicaid Data Reporting

Identifying opportunities to enhance data-driven decision making

Fragmented Data Sources

Limited integration between clinical, financial, and social determinants data creates siloed analysis and incomplete insights for decision-makers.

Reactive vs. Predictive Analytics

Current reporting primarily identifies historical issues rather than forecasting emerging risks or high-cost utilization patterns.

Limited Cohort Analysis

Insufficient tools for analyzing specific member groups across multiple dimensions (clinical, financial, social determinants) hinders targeted intervention design.

Provider Performance Insights

Current provider analytics lack comprehensive views that combine quality metrics, cost efficiency, and population health outcomes.

Key Finding

While AHCA provides valuable reporting through dashboards like the Medicaid Enrollment Dashboard and Quality Initiatives Dashboard, there is significant opportunity to enhance data integration, add predictive capabilities, and provide more detailed drill-down views for targeted population management.
